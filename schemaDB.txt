Cathay accessibility catalog
This is the database schema.
The catalog below is how a passenger's needs are read.
Policy search and chat notes are separate. They do not replace the catalog.

How to read a result
1. The passenger's group is groups.group_id.
2. Needs for that group are needs rows with that group_id.
3. If cathay_has_service is yes, the Cathay service is need_services.service_id, then services and service_actions.
4. If airport_has_service is yes, the facility is needs.airport_service_id, then airport_services, airport_service_actions, and airport_service_legs. Offer it only when the passenger's departure, transit, or arrival airport matches airports and the leg matches.
5. If both flags are no, say Cathay and the airport do not offer it. Still keep the need.
6. Rules come from rule_links for that group_id. If need_id is filled, show the rule only when that need is assigned. show_when is the condition that turns the rule on.
7. A blank source on an action means use the source on the parent service or airport service.

Conventions
- Ids are contiguous integers starting at 1.
- cathay_has_service and airport_has_service are yes or no.
- The name the passenger sees is service_name. On a Cathay need it matches services.service_name. On an airport need it matches airport_services.service_name.
- group_name, rule_name, and the name column on airport_service_legs are copies. Join on the id.
- source is a URL. It is the page with the full instructions.
- Only Hong Kong (HKG, airport_id 1) is loaded. The airport tables can hold more airports.

airports
Primary key: airport_id
Columns: airport_id, iata, name
One row per airport. HKG is Hong Kong International Airport.

airport_services
Primary key: airport_service_id
Foreign key: airport_id -> airports.airport_id
Columns: airport_service_id, airport_id, service_name, description, source
description is the short text to say to the passenger. source is the airport page.

airport_service_actions
Primary key: action_id
Foreign key: airport_service_id -> airport_services.airport_service_id
Columns: action_id, airport_service_id, step, when, stage, who_to_contact, what_to_do, source
step starts at 1 for each airport service. Say the step whose stage matches the leg. A departure leg uses check-in or boarding. A transit or arrival leg uses arrival and transfer.
stage is before travel, check-in, boarding, in flight, or arrival and transfer. One step has one stage.
when is the notice or the moment. who_to_contact is the party, or None. On arrival and transfer it is Airport personnel.
what_to_do is the instruction. source is blank when it is the same page as airport_services.source.

airport_service_legs
Primary key: (airport_service_id, leg)
Foreign key: airport_service_id -> airport_services.airport_service_id
Columns: airport_service_id, name, leg
leg is departure, transit, or arrival. Offer the facility only on those legs.

groups
Primary key: group_id
Columns: group_id, name, acronym
acronym is the short code used when searching policy text.
1 Mobility & Physical, MOB
2 Visual, VIS
3 Hearing, HEAR
4 Cognitive Support, COG
5 Neurodivergence, ND
6 Mental health & emotional, MH
7 Sensory sensitivity, SEN
8 Communication & Language, COM
9 Medical & Health, MED
GEN is not a group. It is only a search filter for a general overview or a complaint.

tags
Primary key: tag_id
Foreign key: group_id -> groups.group_id
Columns: tag_id, group_id, group_name, name, detail
A tag Cathay already uses. Only Mobility, Visual, and Hearing have tags.
WCHC, WCHS, and WCHR are Mobility. Blind is Visual. Deaf is Hearing.
detail says where the attendant stops for WCHS and WCHR. It is blank when the linked services already say what the tag includes.
The other groups have no tag. Their choices are needs, yes or no.

tag_services
Primary key: (tag_id, service_id)
Foreign key: tag_id -> tags.tag_id
Foreign key: service_id -> services.service_id
Columns: tag_id, tag_name, service_id, service_name
The services written once when that tag is saved. tag_name and service_name are copies. Join on the id.
WCHS and WCHR each link only to Airport wheelchair attendant. The destination is tags.detail.

needs
Primary key: need_id
Foreign key: group_id -> groups.group_id
Foreign key: airport_service_id -> airport_services.airport_service_id, only when airport_has_service is yes
Columns: need_id, group_id, group_name, service_name, cathay_has_service, airport_has_service, airport_service_id
One row is one service offered inside one group. The same Cathay service can appear as a separate need in more than one group.
cathay_has_service yes means follow need_services.
airport_has_service yes means follow airport_service_id. airport_service_id is blank otherwise.
Both no means the service is not offered. Sign language and text reminders are the rows in that state.

services
Primary key: service_id
Columns: service_id, source_group, service_name, description, source
A Cathay service, stored once. source_group is the Cathay page it came from, which can differ from the passenger's group.
description is the short text to say to the passenger. source is the Cathay page.

service_actions
Primary key: action_id
Foreign key: service_id -> services.service_id
Columns: action_id, service_id, step, when, stage, who_to_contact, what_to_do, source
step starts at 1 for each service. Say the steps of the current stage in that order.
stage is before travel, check-in, boarding, in flight, or arrival and transfer. One step has one stage. A step that named two moments is stored as two steps.
when is the notice or the moment. who_to_contact is the party, or None. On arrival and transfer it is Airport personnel.
what_to_do is the instruction. source is blank when it is the same page as services.source. Fill it when that step comes from a different page.

need_services
Primary key: (need_id, service_id)
Foreign key: need_id -> needs.need_id
Foreign key: service_id -> services.service_id
Columns: need_id, service_id
Use this only when needs.cathay_has_service is yes. One need has one service. The service text is not copied here.

rules
Primary key: rule_id
Columns: rule_id, name, notice, who_to_contact, applies_when, requirement, source
A limit Cathay states. It is not a service the passenger requests.
requirement is the sentence to say. source is the Cathay page.

rule_links
Primary key: rule_link_id
Foreign key: rule_id -> rules.rule_id
Foreign key: group_id -> groups.group_id
Foreign key: need_id -> needs.need_id, blank when the rule is for the whole group
Columns: rule_link_id, rule_id, rule_name, group_id, group_name, need_id, service_name, show_when
Show the rule when the passenger is in that group and show_when matches their answer.
If need_id is filled, also require that need to be assigned.

Passenger side
These tables hold people and trips. The catalog above is not personal data.
A missing survey answer means the question was not asked. yes and no stay until the passenger changes that answer.

passengers
Primary key: passenger_id
Columns: passenger_id, name, birth_year

profile_groups
Primary key: (passenger_id, group_id)
Foreign key: passenger_id -> passengers.passenger_id
Foreign key: group_id -> groups.group_id
Foreign key: tag_id -> tags.tag_id, blank when this group has no tag
Columns: passenger_id, group_id, tag_id
One row per group the passenger selected. tag_id is WCHC, WCHS, WCHR, Blind, or Deaf.
Cognitive Support, Neurodivergence, Mental health, Sensory sensitivity, Communication, and Medical leave tag_id blank.
Mobility, Visual, and Hearing also leave it blank when the survey produced no tag.

survey_answers
Primary key: one row per passenger and one of service_id, airport_service_id, or need_id
Foreign key: passenger_id -> passengers.passenger_id
Foreign key: service_id -> services.service_id
Foreign key: airport_service_id -> airport_services.airport_service_id
Foreign key: need_id -> needs.need_id
Columns: passenger_id, service_id, airport_service_id, need_id, answer
Exactly one of service_id, airport_service_id, and need_id is filled.
answer is yes or no.
Saving WCHC, Blind, or Deaf writes a yes for each service in tag_services.
Saving WCHS or WCHR writes a yes for Airport wheelchair attendant. The gate or the aircraft door is tags.detail.
need_id is used only for Sign language and Text reminders.
Sunflower lanyard is one row. Courtesy Channel is one row.

bookings
Primary key: booking_id
Foreign key: passenger_id -> passengers.passenger_id
Foreign key: departure_airport_id, transit_airport_id, arrival_airport_id -> airports.airport_id
Columns: booking_id, passenger_id, departure_airport_id, transit_airport_id, arrival_airport_id, departs_at, party_size
A blank transit_airport_id means no transit.
departs_at is how a deadline is calculated. party_size is for a group of 10 or more wheelchairs.

tasks
Primary key: task_id
Foreign key: booking_id -> bookings.booking_id
Columns: task_id, booking_id, what_to_do, due_at, status
status is pending or done.
due_at is blank when Cathay states no deadline.
The seat suggestion is an aisle seat with a movable armrest, not an exit row. It is not stored.
The staff board is page data only. Flights and seats on that board are not a table.

Policy search
These rows are official Cathay and Hong Kong International Airport passages.
They are not passenger data. The text is loaded from a local workbook. That workbook stays off git.

knowledge_chunks
Primary key: chunk_id
Columns: chunk_id, organization, document_title, heading, need_groups, chunk_text, source_url, embedding
chunk_id is text, not an integer.
need_groups is a list of acronyms. A passage can name more than one. Values are the group acronyms, or GEN.
chunk_text is the passage. source_url is the page it came from.
embedding is a 768-number vector used to rank passages. It is not shown to the passenger.
Two indexes: one on need_groups, and one on embedding for similarity search.

Chat notes
chat_messages
Primary key: id
Columns: id, role, content, created_at
A note saved only when someone asks Vera to remember it.
role is user or assistant. content is the note. created_at is the time it was saved.
The open chat stays in the browser. It is not this table.
